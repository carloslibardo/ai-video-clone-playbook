# Prompts de movimento (HeyGen Avatar V)

Escreva em inglês, uma intenção por render. Copie e ajuste.

| Intenção | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Provocar | `Direct intense eye contact, skeptical eyebrow raise, slight firm head shake, open palm questioning gesture.` |
| Resolver | `Warm confident smile, reassuring head nod, open hand gesture toward chest.` |
| Explicar passo a passo | `High energy, upright posture, counting points on fingers, eyes return to the lens after each point.` |
| Segredo / vantagem | `Eyebrows raised, conspiratorial half smile, small lean toward the camera.` |
| Autoridade | `Authoritative nod, precise closed-hand gesture on the key phrase, steady gaze.` |
| Simplicidade | `Relaxed approachable expression, slight smile, effortless open hand gesture.` |
| Deboche (personagem secundário) | `Deadpan, unimpressed, arms relaxed, tiny shrug, looks straight at the lens.` |
| Crise / surpresa (comédia) | `Sudden realization, eyes widen, hands touch own face, then looks at the lens in disbelief.` |
| Com painel ao lado | `Stays frontal, eyes on the lens, gestures stay inside the frame, no head turn.` |
| Fechamento | `Direct charismatic close-up, short pause, warm confident smile held for one second, mouth closed at the end.` |
